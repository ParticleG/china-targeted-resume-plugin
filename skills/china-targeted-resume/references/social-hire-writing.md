# Social-Hire Writing Contract

Use this reference for experienced-hire resumes. The visible resume is a role-specific evidence view, not a career archive and not a capability forecast.

## Selection before wording

For `targeted_application`, include only evidence mapped to the target requirements when mappings exist. Omit unmapped history, generic background, unresolved claims, study-only capabilities, and low-value Preferred material. A record may remain in the private dossier or gap analysis without appearing in the resume.

Keep an otherwise secondary fact only when its owning evidence establishes a direct role connection. State that connection through the demonstrated system, decision, constraint, or outcome; do not add a generic relevance claim.

## Positive, evidence-bounded language

Professional confidence comes from precise scope, decisions, and outcomes. Select the strongest verb supported by the owning evidence:

- `led` / `主导`: the candidate owned the material decision and outcome;
- `owned` / `负责`: the candidate carried continuing accountability for a named boundary;
- `designed` / `设计`: the candidate made the stated design decision;
- `implemented` / `实现`: the candidate performed the named implementation;
- `drove` / `推动`: the candidate primarily resolved delivery or dependency friction;
- `coordinated` / `协调`: the candidate connected named parties without claiming their work;
- `contributed` / `参与`: follow immediately with the exact personal contribution.

Do not upgrade a contribution verb, remove a team-result qualifier, broaden a metric population, or convert adjacent experience into direct practice. Short-term learnability may support an `apply_with_risks` recommendation or a roadmap handoff; it never turns study, a plan, or expected future work into present evidence.

## Terminology and jargon

Retain conventional terms that make the fact more exact: QPS, P99, SLO, RTO/RPO, GMV, DAU, conversion rate, A/B test, retrieval, attribution, idempotency, backpressure, or a named technology. Use target-JD vocabulary only when it is semantically equivalent to the evidence owner.

Translate low-information jargon into an object, action, and result. Terms such as `赋能`, `抓手`, `闭环`, `打法`, `拉通`, `沉淀`, or `降本增效` are acceptable only when the same sentence names what changed, the candidate's boundary, and the verified outcome. If deleting the term loses no fact, delete it.

## Experienced-hire bullet shape

Prefer one of these evidence-preserving shapes:

1. `Outcome: decision/action; measured scope and personal boundary.`
2. `Problem under constraint; personal decision; verified result.`
3. `Owned boundary; cross-team or system scope; operational result and qualifier.`

A strong bullet usually answers at least three of: what problem, what the candidate did, why that approach, what changed, how it was measured, and where the candidate's responsibility ended.

Use a two-layer sentence for mixed recruiter and hiring-manager readers: start with the business or operational outcome, then name the technical/product mechanism and measurement.

## Metrics and seniority signals

Preserve baseline, result, time window, environment, population, benchmark conditions, and team-versus-personal attribution when the source provides them. Never present an offline benchmark as an online production result.

Show seniority through verified scope rather than adjectives: system or product boundary, decision ownership, migration and rollback responsibility, dependency count, team scope, operational risk, failure handling, and sustained accountability.

## Near-match requirements and independent constraints

An explicit unsatisfied application constraint hard gate—such as work authorization, a mandatory license, or a non-negotiable location rule—remains blocking. Never average it away with capability evidence.

Experience duration and skill thresholds remain explicit requirements. The requirement side is authoritative only when parsed from one atomic numeric verbatim quote/span. The candidate side is authoritative only when a current `EvidenceRecord` is rebuilt from an owning path/hash/span and its extractive safe claim contains one atomic duration plus an explicit checked date. A binding must exactly match the requirement-owned scope/minimum/maximum and every referenced record's candidate scope/years/check time. Reject arbitrary selected IDs, self-reported numbers, records without duration facts, altered thresholds, compound scopes, free text, inferred requirements, missing audit data, or mismatched evidence. A verified shortfall of at most 25% may yield `apply_with_risks`; the requirement remains unmet and the rationale must retain scope, evidence IDs, and check time.

Small capability gaps already belong in the gap and interview-risk analysis. They may support applying with risks; they never authorize invented resume claims.
