# China Recruiting Context

Use this reference to adapt presentation for candidate-side applications in China without changing facts.

## Presentation priorities

- Default to concise `zh-CN` wording when the user asks for a Chinese resume; retain English technology names where conventional.
- Put name, target headline, appropriate contact details, concise summary, skills, experience, selected projects, education/honors, and verified links in a fast-scanning one-column order.
- Use explicit date ranges, organization, title/role, location or work mode only when verified, and clear personal action/result bullets.
- Front-load recent, role-relevant systems and project evidence. Compress older or low-relevance details rather than inflating keywords.
- Treat education, language, location, work authorization, travel, availability, and certifications as independent constraints when a source makes them material.

## Targeting without fabrication

Adapt by selection, ordering, compression, and truthful terminology. Use JD language only where semantically equivalent to owning evidence. Make adjacent-domain transfer explainable rather than presenting it as direct experience. Keep real employer, title, responsibility, stage, and metric scope unchanged.

Company research may influence vocabulary, emphasis, project ranking, interview questions, and risk notes. It does not create a candidate achievement. Avoid company flattery in the resume body; keep the company target primarily in metadata/file naming unless a truthful summary needs it.

## Recruiting-text interpretation

Preserve explicit Required versus Preferred wording and responsibilities. Repetition is not a hard-gate signal. Flag stale postings, contradictory seniority, implausibly broad stacks, unclear employment arrangement, ambiguous location, missing salary or reporting scope, and copied descriptions as analysis risks rather than candidate facts.

## Privacy and audience

A direct application is not public publication. Confirm P2 use for the exact target and purpose. Public portfolio output normally omits phone and excludes P2. Remove internal/client/product secrets and avoid exposing private source paths in the visible resume; keep provenance in private audit artifacts.

Do not infer demographic attributes or add sensitive personal fields merely because some local resume conventions use them. Include only user-authorized, role-relevant information supported by the source policy.
