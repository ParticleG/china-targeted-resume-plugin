# Service Coordination Role

## What success looks like

The role coordinates operational reviews, communicates status, and keeps action records current. The requirement list intentionally has no Required or Preferred heading.

- Coordinate recurring review sessions and publish decision notes.
- Explain service status to technical and non-technical partners.
- Track follow-up actions through closure.
